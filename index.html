Emergency information

Tap a number below to call from a phone in Italy.

🆘112General emergencyCall 112
🚑118Ambulance / medicalCall 118
👮113State PoliceCall 113
🚒115Fire serviceCall 115
If unsure, call 112. Say "Emergenza. Parla inglese?" and give the address: Via dei Leutari 29, Roma.
These numbers dial correctly on an Israeli roaming SIM too — emergency calls always route through the local Italian network, no country code needed.
